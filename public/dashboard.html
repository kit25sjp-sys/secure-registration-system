Password Policy & Forbidden Examples

The system will reject any password that matches these patterns. Do not use passwords like these.

🔒 Common / default passwords
  • password
  • password123
  • admin123
  • 123456789
  • iloveyou
  • welcome

These appear in every leaked-credentials database and are tried first in any attack.

⌨ Keyboard patterns & sequences
  • qwerty
  • qwerty123
  • asdfgh
  • 123456
  • abcdef
  • zxcvbn

Keyboard walks and numeric runs are trivially guessable with automated tools.

👥 Personal / obvious words
  • yourname123
  • michael
  • baseball
  • superman
  • dragon
  • sunshine

Dictionary words and names are broken instantly by dictionary attacks.

🔁 Repeated characters
  • aaaaaaa
  • 1111111
  • aabbcc
  • aaabbb
  • pppppp
  • 000000

Repeated characters drastically reduce entropy and fail strength checks.

✓ What a strong password looks like
  • Tr0ub4dor&3
  • P@ssw0rd!2026
  • X7#kLm!9qR
  • Secure#Rain42!
  • Cy8er$Vault#99

Mix uppercase, lowercase, numbers & symbols. Minimum 8 chars — 12+ recommended.

📝 Password requirements
  • ✓ At least 8 characters (12+ recommended)
  • ✓ At least one uppercase letter (A–Z)
  • ✓ At least one lowercase letter (a–z)
  • ✓ At least one number (0–9)
  • ✓ At least one special character (!@#$%…)
  • ✗ Must not be a common/dictionary word
  • ✗ Must not reuse any of your last 5 passwords
